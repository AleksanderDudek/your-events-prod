Claude Community Wrocław ​[Event in English 🇬🇧] 🔴 Registration is only on Luma: https://luma.com/claude-1w9p 🔴 An RSVP here does NOT reserve a seat. Every request is approved by hand on Luma. Welcome to the fifth Claude Community Wrocław meetup, a community-driven initiative built by builders, for builders https://claudecommunity.pl/ ​We share real stories of how we use Claude, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork in our daily work. No product pitches, no vendor talk, just honest experience from people who build with these tools every day. ​The event is free and pizza and drinks are included. If you wish to speak at our future events, use this form: Open Google Form ​🟢 Where and when? Barbara (Wrocław Institute of Culture), ul. Świdnicka 8B, 50-067 Wrocław Tuesday 17 November, 6PM CET 🔴 the location is different than usual 🔴 ​Agenda ➡️ 18:00 - Welcome & intro by Claude Community Wrocław organizers 🧑‍💻 18:05 - Say hello to Claude Code Mods - Daniel Aniszkiewicz 🧑‍💻 18:25 - The Documentation Your AI Actually Needs: Lessons from a Spec-Driven Full-Stack Rewrite - Veronica Marin Kramer 🍕 19:05 - Networking & Pizza 🧑‍💻 19:35 - I gave Claude a napkin sketch of a house - Przemysław Nowak ​➡️ Say hello to Claude Code Mods ​Mods let Claude Code change how it looks and how it behaves, which is new. I'll cover what they are and show a few I built, some useful and some just for fun. ​🎤 Daniel Aniszkiewicz \- Claude Community Ambassador for Poland; AWS Security Hero; Senior Software Engineer ​➡
